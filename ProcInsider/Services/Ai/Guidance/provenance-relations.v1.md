# Provenance and relations
Evidence belongs to a case, evidence session, capture, host, source identity and execution root. Submission hints and annotation snapshots are navigation context, not checked observations. Inspect returned coverage, source run, source revision and omissions before drawing conclusions. Source identity and execution-root filtering constrain counts; only unrestricted checked coverage permits a capture-wide claim. An empty bounded page, partial source, denied query or unavailable Tool does not establish absence.
Use exact checked reference IDs from this execution for case claims and pivots. Durable process identity is ProcessEntityId; PID alone is not identity. Record timestamps may represent collection, occurrence or processing time; retain their meaning and UTC bounds. Relations carry their own provenance and qualification; co-occurrence is not causation or verified ownership. State ambiguity and conflicting observations rather than merging them into a false identity.
GetSnapshotEvidenceRelations, when offered, pages recorded decisions touching an exact checked object; Active and Superseded decisions are both included. Inspect From/To, role, correlation state/confidence, candidates, resolver and status before describing a link. Endpoint IDs are recorded claims, not independently resolved originals or causal proof. GetSnapshotSourceRunLineage requires a checked producer-run reference and pages its scoped stored parent/input artifact, path and hash metadata. Parents and inputs are not independently resolved or opened on disk. Returned lineage references permit exact Details round-trips without adopting IDs from old turns. Retain missing-source/schema/continuation coverage explicitly.
