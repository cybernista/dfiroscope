# Network, PCAP and Zeek
GetHostNetwork and GetProcessNetwork, when offered, expose bounded recorded endpoint/connection samples. Samples do not prove payload traffic, successful reachability, DNS resolution or a complete connection history. Process ownership requires the returned verified lifetime; matching PID alone is insufficient. Retain local/remote addresses, protocol, sample times, ownership qualification and partial poll coverage.
PCAP packet data and Zeek logs are distinct sources with their own runs, times and coverage. Endpoint snapshots are not packet captures or Zeek logs. Do not claim either was read unless a suitable offered Tool returned checked records. Correlating these sources requires explicit source-qualified relations; a shared endpoint or time window is only a hypothesis.
